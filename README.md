# 🛠️ Campus Fix
**Bridging the gap between students and campus maintenance through real-time, intelligent reporting.**

---

## 📌 Overview
University living should be about learning, not worrying about broken furniture or infrastructure failures. **Campus Fix** is a modern web-based platform designed to replace outdated manual logs with a streamlined, digital-first approach to dormitory maintenance. 

By empowering students to report issues in real-time and providing administrators with intelligent management tools, we ensure that campus infrastructure is safe, functional, and reliable.

---

## 🛑 The Problem
Current dormitory management relies on informal or manual reporting methods, such as verbal complaints or paper-based logs. This leads to:
* **Delayed Responses:** Maintenance issues often go unaddressed for extended periods.
* **Information Loss:** Manual logs are easily misplaced, leading to a lack of accountability.
* **Zero Transparency:** Students have no visibility into the status of their reported issues.
* **Safety & Performance Risks:** Unresolved infrastructure problems compromise student health, safety, and academic focus.

---

## ✅ The Solution
**Campus Fix** provides a centralized hub for facility management:
* **Real-time Reporting:** Students can submit detailed reports of infrastructure issues instantly.
* **Progress Tracking:** Users receive live updates and can track the lifecycle of their requests.
* **Admin Dashboard:** Equipped with intelligent tools to help administrators respond more efficiently.
* **Enhanced Communication:** Effectively bridges the gap between the student body and campus administration.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Future Roadmap
We are building more than just a reporting tool; we are building an intelligent ecosystem.

1.  **🤖 AI-Powered Support (RAG):** Reports are analyzed by the Campus Fix AI Agent. It recommends immediate fixing strategies to students for minor issues; if the problem persists, it is escalated to Admins.
2.  **⚙️ Smart Task Allocation:** The system automatically assigns technical personnel to specific tasks while maintaining the flexibility for manual assignment.
3.  **💬 Interactive Experience:** Focused on high-level interaction for both students and administrators using **WebSockets** for real-time updates.
4.  **🔐 Enterprise-Grade Security:** Secured authentication using **Google OAuth** and **Express Session**.
5.  **📈 Scalable Architecture:** Built as a reliable system that can scale from a single dormitory to a large-scale university network.
