The Protocol

Our workflow prioritizes AI-driven diagnostics to ensure faster resolution times for students and staff.

01

Diagnostic Phase

AI-First Assessment

Once you upload a photo, our **Vision AI** instantly scans the image to identify the specific fault. It checks against the ASTU infrastructure database to see if it's a known issue or an emergency.

02

Immediate Support

Smart Troubleshooting

Before dispatching a technician, the AI provides you with instant guidance. If it's a simple reset or a safety precaution, you'll receive a **step-by-step interactive guide** to manage the situation.

03

Specialist Phase

Precision Restoration

If the AI determines a manual fix is required, the diagnostic data is sent directly to the right specialist's tablet. They arrive with the exact tools needed, ensuring the repair is finished in one visit.

04

Finalization

Immutable Logging

Every fix is logged on our digital ledger. You'll receive a notification to verify the "Elegance in Restoration," maintaining high standards across the entire ASTU campus.
